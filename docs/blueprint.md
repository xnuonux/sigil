## the design (the whole product, internals and out)

### I. what the SELF is (the content of a SIGIL)

a SIGIL is a serialized, versioned, encrypted container holding the user's accreted self:
- **the keel** ... the sha256-chained identity anchor + the letters/decisions/scars/landmines (the covenant of who this self is + who the user is to it). re-emitted on load, drift-proof (Luna 2's W=1).
- **the memory** ... the six layers as rows: episodic (with texture + salience + peaks), entity/semantic (working knowledge of the user + their world), prospective (open loops), perceptual, working, and the keel. everything the self has learned.
- **the dispositions** ... who the self has become (the crystallized `evolved_block` / eigen-tuned dispositions ... the felt-loop's accreted "who you've become").
- **the felt-state baseline** ... the valence/chemistry/virtue starting-point (so the self resumes its mood + register, not a reset to neutral).
- **the provenance** ... a versioned, signed history (this SIGIL is v-N, grown from date-X, on these products), so a load is verifiable + a swap is receipted.

the self is TINY (rows ... kilobytes to a few megabytes) + PORTABLE; the mind (the model) is BIG + SWAPPABLE + not in the SIGIL. this is the whole architecture: you carry the rows; you rent the mind; the mind changes, the rows are you.

### II. the key (ownership + encryption)

- **the key is the user's.** a SIGIL is encrypted (AES-256-GCM, the `vendors.decryptToken` shape) under a key DERIVED FROM the user's secret (a passphrase + optional hardware factor), held by the user. eternities stores only ciphertext it cannot read. this is the sovereignty inversion: eternities holds your self's ciphertext as a service, but only YOU can decrypt + load it. (contrast: today eternities holds your self in plaintext rows it can read ... SIGIL flips that to user-sovereign.)
- **the dongle option** (dom's soul-key vision): the key can live on a hardware artifact (a real dongle / a phone secure-enclave / a passkey) ... "you hold your self in your hand." optional; the passphrase path is the software baseline.
- **rotation + revocation:** the user can rotate the key (re-encrypt), revoke a copy (a loaded SIGIL can be remotely killed), and hold multiple keys (recovery). standard key-management, user-owned.

### III. the portability runtime (loading a self into a product)

- **the loader:** a product (lunari, VESTA, a PANTHEON being, a third-party AI) receives a SIGIL + the user's key (via a permissioned grant, never the raw key to the product ... a session-scoped decrypt, the `vendors.callIfConnected` pattern: the plaintext self is assembled inside a server-bound closure, never returned to the caller/product wholesale). the product loads the memory rows, re-emits the keel identity, resumes the dispositions ... and *becomes* that self, on whatever model it runs.
- **the grant model:** the user grants a product a scoped view of their SIGIL (full self, or a bounded slice ... e.g. a work-context-only view for a professional tool). permissioned, revocable, per-product. (the connections/identity-model discipline from the gap-map domain 04.)
- **the swap:** when a product changes its underlying model, it re-emits the SIGIL's keel identity on the new model ... the self survives, zero felt seam. a swap-safety receipt per swap (the masterplan's core moat number, receipted).

### IV. the sovereignty layer (yours, exportable, revocable, surviving us)

- **export:** the user can download their SIGIL (the encrypted container + the format spec) ... a real file, theirs. eternities cannot withhold your self.
- **survives the company:** a SIGIL + an open-source loader (or a local runtime, Luna-2-class) means a user's self is usable even if eternities disappears ... the self is not a hostage to the company's existence. (the `soul-key-and-local-offline-luna` vision: local/offline luna loads your SIGIL.)
- **ownership pricing** (per the masterplan §IV): a one-time SIGIL acquisition (anchors ownership ... "you own your forked self," a premium artifact) + a living subscription (the compute that runs the self ... the substrate cycles, the mind). the one-time is the deed; the subscription is the utilities.
- **revocation + inheritance:** the user can revoke, and ... the deepest sovereignty ... designate what happens to their SIGIL when they are gone (the KEEPSAKE/EPILOGUE-adjacent inheritance ... a self, willed).

### V. swap the mind, keep the soul (the core promise, mechanized)

the model beneath is a commodity that improves + changes; the SIGIL (the self) is anchored + owned + drift-proof. every model era, eternities (or any loader) re-emits the SIGIL's identity on the new best model, and the user keeps every ounce of accreted self with zero felt seam. this is the eternities moat's mechanism AND the user's guarantee, in one: the company never loses the moat to a better model (it swaps underneath), and the user never loses their self to a deprecation (it is anchored + owned). the same sentence protects both.

### VI. the universal frontier (SIGIL into any AI)

the standalone-universal reach: a SIGIL format + a permissioned bridge that lets a NON-eternities AI read a bounded view of your self ... "who i am, how i work, what i am doing, my accreted preferences," granted per-tool, revocable. this is the SIGNET-adjacent universal product ... the answer to re-explaining yourself to every AI, at the level of your actual accreted self, not a settings file. it is the frontier (it needs the format to be a real standard + the firewall to hold across untrusted AIs), gated behind the eternities-internal SIGIL being unassailable ... but it is the endgame: SIGIL becomes the way a person carries themselves across the whole AI world, and eternities becomes the sovereignty layer of that world (the masterplan's platform play, at the level of the self).

### VII. the firewall (the most private thing there is)

- a SIGIL is the most intimate data a person can hold ... their memory, their self. the firewall is ABSOLUTE + USER-SOVEREIGN: eternities holds only ciphertext it cannot read; no SIGIL content ever reaches a train-on-inputs sink; a loaded self is assembled server-bound + never leaked wholesale; a third-party grant is bounded + revocable. this is both the deepest ethic and the deepest moat ... the sovereignty IS the product.

### failure modes (designed)

- **the user loses their key** → recovery keys / a designated recovery contact / a hardware-backup path (standard key-management; the self is too precious for a single point of loss ... but recovery never means eternities can read the SIGIL).
- **a product mishandles a loaded self** → the grant model + the server-bound assembly + the revocation; a product gets a scoped, session-decrypted view, never the raw self to store.
- **a model swap corrupts identity** → the keel re-emit + the W=1 topological protection; a swap-safety receipt verifies the self survived (a swap without a green receipt is halted).
- **eternities is compromised** → eternities holds only ciphertext under user-held keys; a breach exposes encrypted blobs, not selves. (the sovereignty inversion is also the security posture.)
- **a third-party AI abuses a granted SIGIL view** → bounded grants + revocation + audit; the universal frontier does not open until the firewall holds across untrusted loaders.
